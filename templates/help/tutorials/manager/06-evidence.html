What it means

The RePORTER identity

The first line of Grants says which NIH RePORTER profile is this PI's: not evaluated yet, resolved (found automatically), pinned by staff, staff confirmed: no RePORTER profile, or another state that ends no RePORTER grants are shown. Unless it is resolved, a manager sees the candidate profiles with checkboxes, a RePORTER profile id field, This is the PI and PI has no RePORTER profile. A pin or a "no profile" mark offers Unpin.

{% call careful("Careful: This is the PI, PI has no RePORTER profile, Unpin") %}

This is the PI pins the chosen RePORTER profiles; PI has no RePORTER profile shows no RePORTER grants for this PI. Each records a persona revision and queues a refresh of the PI's grants. Unpin removes the pin or the mark and lets the automatic match run again.

{% endcall %}

NIH RePORTER awards and ORCID fundings

The awards list shows each award's title, project number, activity code, fiscal years and the evidence that linked it to this PI. The tenure filter line says whether awards are limited to the organisation only (no JHU tenure start recorded) or to the organisation and fiscal years from the tenure start on. A line above the lists counts the active and past grants in the persona. The ORCID fundings list below shows the fundings on the PI's ORCID record. Each award or funding offers Not this PI; a vetoed one is greyed out and marked vetoed.

{% call careful("Careful: Not this PI") %}

Not this PI takes the award or funding out of the persona at once, and later refreshes keep it out. It cannot be undone: no button brings a vetoed grant or funding back.

{% endcall %}

Industry interest

The section shows the PI's cohort percentile, the raw score, the number of evidence rows and, for a manager, how each kind of evidence contributed. It may instead say Not computed yet, Rescoring (the scorer changed and a new score is on its way) or Unscored with the reason. A partial tag means some evidence sources were unavailable or truncated. The score is for reading only: it never enters the profile or the lab bot's prompts.

Evidence opens the list of evidence rows: company, kind, year, the PI's role and the source. Each row offers Not this PI / not industry.

{% call careful("Careful: Not this PI / not industry") %}

This veto removes the evidence row from the score, and the PI is rescored at once. It cannot be undone: no button brings a vetoed row back.

{% endcall %}

Companies

The companies the PI founded or co-founded, advises, or serves as a board member. Only Confirmed entries reach BlackbirdBot's profile lookup and the assessment pages; a suggestion reaches neither until a manager confirms it.

  • Add a company takes a company name, the PI's role, optional funding in US dollars with the date it was true, and a source link. An entry added by hand is confirmed at once.
  • Suggested lists what discovery found in the PI's own competing-interest statements and in Wikidata, with funding from SEC Form D filings where one matched. Each suggestion can be corrected (role, funding, or Clear funding) and then Confirmed, or Rejected.
  • Delete removes a confirmed company, after a confirmation dialog.
  • Find companies queues discovery for this PI. While it runs the button is disabled, and the line under it can say discovery is waiting for the daily extraction budget, with the time it resumes.
{% call careful("Careful: Add company and Confirm") %}

An added or confirmed company becomes visible to BlackbirdBot and on the assessment pages straight away. Delete takes it back off.

{% endcall %} {% call careful("Careful: Reject and Delete") %}

A deleted company leaves BlackbirdBot's view at once (a suggestion never reached it). Both are recorded as rejected, and discovery never suggests them again; the only way back is to add the company by hand. Delete asks first; Reject does not.

{% endcall %} {% call careful("Careful: Find companies") %}

Find companies queues a discovery job in the background. Its suggestions appear on this page when it finishes; nothing reaches BlackbirdBot until a suggestion is confirmed.

{% endcall %}
