Where

Workspace → Landscape, and from there the pipeline page.

What it means

Landscape opportunities come from a pipeline that reads Johns Hopkins research, keeps the strongest therapeutic ideas and has BlackbirdBot assess them. Each week everyone gets the same batch, and records a quick decision on each card: pursue or pass. A manager decides like a reviewer does; the Reviewer tutorial walks through the batch and the decision.

Current and All assessed

Managers also get Current, every opportunity in the current cut, and All assessed, every opportunity with an assessment. Their Unreviewed and Reviewed tabs are yours alone: they say whether you have decided. Until you decide on an opportunity you see nobody else's decisions or reviews of it, as a reviewer does, and while you have undecided cards in a blind batch both lists leave that batch's cards out.

The pipeline page

It shows the phase and whether the pipeline is paused, the sets of judging criteria and their checks, each source's intake, the running counts and spend, the latest cut, the reports, and the items each stage filtered out.

  • Pause and Resume stop and restart new work.
  • Rescue returns a filtered-out item to the pipeline, with your reason.
  • Positive controls lists papers behind Blackbird's past positive decisions, so the pipeline can report where each was dropped.
  • Experts marks the accounts that judge sample opportunities in full.
{% call careful("Careful: pipeline controls") %}

Each control asks you to confirm, then acts at once for everyone. Pause and Resume undo each other, and the positive controls and experts can be changed back; a rescue is recorded with your name and reason and cannot be undone from the page.

{% endcall %}

Only an admin can use Start backfill, Re-run bake-off and Override, which lets a held set of criteria go ahead.
