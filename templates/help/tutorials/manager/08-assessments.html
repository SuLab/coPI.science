Where

Workspace → Assessments → detail → on any assessment. The Assigned block is at the end of the Human review section, just before the Interview timeline.

What you'll see

{{ figure(request, "assign-to", "The Assigned block on an assessment's detail page, with Assign to.") }}

What it means

A manager reads and reviews assessments as a reviewer does, and can also assign them.

Assigning

  • Assigned lists who is assigned, or says No one assigned. A manager sees Remove beside each name.
  • Assign to picks from every admin, manager and reviewer account whose access is approved, and Assign adds that person. Assigning someone twice changes nothing.
  • On the Assessments list, the Assignment menu's Assigned to me narrows the list to your own assignments, and each card names who is assigned.

An assignment is workflow metadata only. It grants no access, since reviewers can already read every assessment, and Blackbird sends no notification, so the person hears of it only from you. It also does not count as a review: the list's Reviewed tab means written feedback exists, not that someone is assigned.{% if permissions.admin %} Assign and Remove are hidden while an admin is viewing the site as someone else.{% endif %}

{% call careful("Careful: Assign and Remove") %}

Assign and Remove change who is listed on the assessment for everyone who opens it. Each undoes the other.
