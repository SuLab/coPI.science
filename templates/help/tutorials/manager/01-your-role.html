What it means

This tutorial is a read-only tour of the pages a manager uses: the PIs and their lab bots, staff accounts for reviewers, and the records the simulation leaves behind. Each step says where a page is, shows it, and explains what it means. Nothing in the tutorial changes any data.

{% call note("How to read this tutorial") %}

Some controls on the real pages change things for real: they create accounts, contact Slack, put a lab bot on the running simulation or remove evidence for good. Every step that describes one introduces it with a Careful box saying what happens and whether a page in Blackbird can undo it. The tutorial never asks you to press any of them.

{% endcall %}

Managers, reviewers and admins

In one line each, and filled in by the rest of this tutorial:

  • Reviewer: views PI research and reviews assessments; assignments are workflow metadata. A reviewer's Workspace has only the PIs and Assessments tabs, and the staff pages answer "Manager access required".
  • Manager: manages PI research and lab operations, and reviews assessments like a reviewer. A manager adds PIs, reviews and corrects their profiles and evidence, installs, activates and mutes their lab bots, assigns assessments, adds reviewer accounts, and reads the Discussions, Activity and Prompt Suggestions pages.
  • Admin: every manager feature, plus account, agent and system administration.

A manager account has no research profile and no lab bot of its own, so it has no My Profile or My Agent page and skips the PI onboarding pages.

What only an admin can do

These come up later where they limit what a manager can do. Only an admin can:

  • create a manager account, or change an admin or manager account;
  • revoke a reviewer who has never signed in;
  • override the profile checks that refuse to activate a lab bot;
  • suspend a lab bot, or manage one that is suspended;
  • delete a PI's account;
  • delete review feedback;
  • export Discussions, read a specialist's reply verbatim, and see a run's model-call logs.

The concepts behind these pages (lab agents, simulation runs, interviews, the specialist panel and assessments) are explained in How Blackbird works.
