Where

Workspace → Landscape.

What it means

Landscape opportunities come from reading research from Scripps Research, not from lab interviews. Each week the same new batch is issued to everyone, and your job is a quick decision on each card: pursue or pass.

The page

  • This week's batch comes first, in the pipeline's order, strongest first, with no rank number shown. Tiles above it count the cards by recommendation.
  • Below it are the cards from earlier batches that you have not decided on yet. They stay until you decide, so a missed week is never lost.
  • A card marked changed since your decision has a rewritten assessment: read it, then confirm or change your decision.

A card

{{ figure(request, "landscape-card", "A Landscape card: the recommendation, In one minute, the cost line and key points, and the quick decision.") }}

Each card has the headline, which opens the opportunity's page, the structured line of each idea, the labs and BlackbirdBot's recommendation. In one minute gives the pitch, with detail → to the page; beside it, under Key points, come the cost line in bold and the key points, each risk marked Risk:. Why this score opens to show the score and band. At the foot are the counts of red flags and of each confirmed deal-breaker, Assigned:, and, once you have decided, Decided by:.

Deciding

Under Quick decision on each card, choose Pursue or Pass, pick any reason tags that fit under Reason tags (optional), add a short reason, and press Record decision. You can edit your decision later. Until you have decided on an opportunity you see nobody else's decisions or reviews of it, and no counts of them, so your decision is your own. A full review, with a rating from 1 to 5 and a comment of up to 10,000 characters, is optional and is on the opportunity's page.

{% call careful() %}

Your decision is saved under your name, and others who have decided on the same opportunity can read it.

{% endcall %}

Blind batches

Some weeks the batch is blind: shuffled rather than in the pipeline's order. Until you decide on a card, it hides the recommendation, the score and band and the flag counts, and the opportunity's page hides the score, band and dimension scores. Decide from the headline, the pitch, the cost line, the key points and the evidence on the opportunity's page.

{% call note("Expert opportunities") %}

If a manager has marked you as an expert, your Landscape page also lists a few opportunities for you to judge in full. The pipeline's own calls on them stay hidden until you submit.

{% endcall %}
