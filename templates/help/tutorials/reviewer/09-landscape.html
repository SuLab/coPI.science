Where

Workspace → Landscape.

What it means

Landscape opportunities come from reading Johns Hopkins research, not from lab interviews. Each week the same new batch is issued to everyone, and your job is a quick decision on each card: pursue or pass.

The page

  • This week's batch comes first, strongest first.
  • Below it are the cards from earlier batches that you have not decided on yet. They stay until you decide, so a missed week is never lost.
  • A card marked changed since your decision has a rewritten assessment: read it, then confirm or change your decision.

Deciding

On each card, choose Pursue or Pass, pick any reason tags that fit, and add a short reason. You can edit your decision later. Until you have decided on an opportunity you see nobody else's decisions or reviews of it, and no counts of them, so your decision is your own. A full review, with a rating from 1 to 5 and a comment of up to 10,000 characters, is optional and is on the opportunity's page.

{% call careful() %}

Your decision is saved under your name, and others who have decided on the same opportunity can read it.

{% endcall %}

Blind batches

Some weeks the batch is blind: shuffled rather than ranked, with the score, band and their rationales hidden until you decide on each card, and the ranks until the next batch is issued. Decide from the headline, the key points and the evidence.

{% call note("Expert opportunities") %}

If a manager has marked you as an expert, your Landscape page also lists a few opportunities for you to judge in full. The pipeline's own calls on them stay hidden until you submit.
