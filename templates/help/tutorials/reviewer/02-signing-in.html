What it means

Signing in

Sign in with ORCID, using the ORCID iD your account was created with. If you opened a link to a page before signing in, that page opens once you are signed in. Otherwise you land on the Assessments list.

{% if microsoft_sign_in %}

You can also add your organisation's Microsoft account as a second way to sign in. You link it in Settings, under Microsoft sign-in, after confirming it's you by signing in with ORCID again. Your first sign-in is always with ORCID, and ORCID keeps working after you link. Unlink Microsoft account signs you out everywhere. See Microsoft sign-in.

{% endif %}

Finding your way

  • Settings holds your own account options, including Delete account{% if microsoft_sign_in %} and Microsoft sign-in{% endif %}.
  • Workspace opens the pages you work in. Its row of tabs shows PIs and Assessments.
  • Help holds this tutorial, the reference pages, a Glossary and Troubleshooting.
  • Sign out signs you out on every device, not only this browser.

Workspace pages also have a Help with this page link in their header. It opens the part of the reference that explains that page.

The "New to Blackbird?" box

Until you answer it, a box at the top of each Workspace page offers this tutorial. Start the tutorial opens the first step and records nothing, so the box keeps appearing. Don't show again hides it for good and leaves you on the page you were reading. Pressing Finish on this tutorial's last step hides it too. The tutorials stay under Help either way.

More detail: Signing in, Your first sign-in, Finding your way around, Settings and Signing out.
