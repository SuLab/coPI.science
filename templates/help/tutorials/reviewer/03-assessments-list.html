What it means

The list is your work queue. Each card is one assessment: one verdict BlackbirdBot wrote at the end of one interview.

The sentence under the heading

It names the rubric version in use and prints the band lines: the weighted score a verdict needs for advance, for conditional, and the label used below that. Read the thresholds there; they come from the rubric and can change. The weighted score is computed by the app from the dimension scores, not taken from the model.

The filters

The controls at the top right change what the list shows. They take effect when you press Apply.

  • Run: which simulation run to show. The latest run is selected at first and is marked "— current". Each option also shows the run's status, how many assessments it stored and its rubric version. All Runs shows every run on record.
  • Sort: Score (triage), Most recent, Recommendation or Lab.
  • Assignment: All, or Assigned to me for only the assessments assigned to you.
  • Lab: All labs, or one lab.

When more assessments match than the page shows, a line under the heading says so: it shows the top of the current sort order, and the rest sit further down that order.

The tabs

Unreviewed, Reviewed and All each show a count. You land on Unreviewed. Reviewed means someone has written feedback on the assessment. Being assigned to someone does not make it reviewed, and neither does an approval decision. The counts follow the run, lab and assignment you have selected.

The banners

  • An amber banner counts verdicts lost: verdicts that were generated and never stored, or never produced at all. It lists each reason with a short explanation. None of them can be recovered.
  • An orange banner counts verdicts with an incomplete, unverified or unrecorded specialist panel. Those verdicts were stored but not fully vetted: treat their scores as provisional, and read each card's panel badge to see which case applies. This count covers every lab and assignment in the run, so it can be larger than what your filters show.

The summary and the distribution

The summary cards count the assessments shown below by the hub's recommendation: Total, Advance, Conditional, Route-to-incubation and Decline.

Dimension distribution, when you open it, lists each rubric dimension with its weight, the specialist who covers it, and how the shown assessments scored on it. It also counts the bands. It follows the tab you are on.

A short legend explains the gating symbols: met, not met, and unconfirmed (the question was never answered, which is not the same as a gate that failed). The symbols themselves are inside each card, as the next step shows.
