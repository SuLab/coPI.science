What it means

The detail page holds everything stored for one verdict. A row of links (Brief, Evidence, Panel, Gating, Scores, Review, Timeline, plus Ask and Rationale when the verdict has them) stays at the top as you scroll and jumps to each section. Expand all and Collapse all open or close every collapsed section at once. The sections, in page order:

Brief

The headline with the hub's confidence, then In one minute and Key points side by side, and Why this score when the hub wrote one. When a manager has confirmed companies for the PI, they are listed as Companies (staff-confirmed), with the date the list was taken from. The brief is BlackbirdBot's own summary of the verdict, not an independent account.

Evidence summary

The stored verdict sorted into Strengths, Risks, Mid-scale (dimensions that are neither) and Not established. Inside each, rows are grouped by where they come from: dimension scores, gating criteria, red flags and specialist consults. A "Hub's reason:" line is the hub's own reason; a "Rubric definition:" line is the rubric's text, shown when the hub gave no reason. show opens a row's quoted text.

The ask

The next experiment the hub recommends, when it recorded one. Show the full plan opens the rest.

Verdict

The lab, the agent that screened it, the date and the Slack channel. On the right, the Hub recommendation and the Score and band, which the app computes rather than takes from the model, with that revision's band lines. A line below names the rubric revision that scored the verdict. Scores from different revisions are not directly comparable.

Specialist panel

A banner that is always open. It reads Specialist panel: no gap recorded, Specialist panel incomplete (with the domains never consulted), Specialist panel unverified, Specialist panel: not required or Specialist panel: not recorded, with a sentence saying what that state means. Only "no gap recorded" says that nothing the verdict needed from a specialist was left unconsulted. Chips below summarise each domain's signals. "No gap recorded" is only about coverage: it says nothing about what the specialists found. A consulted specialist can still signal gap or blocking, as the domain chips below the banner show.

Gating criteria

Closed at first. Each gate shows met, not met or unconfirmed, the rubric's definition where it is known and, when recorded, the hub's reason. "Not met" (asked and failed) and "unconfirmed" (never asked) are different answers; only the first can justify discounting an idea.

Red flags

Always open, because these are warnings. "None recorded." when there are none.

Full rationale

The hub's full reasoning, when it wrote one. Read it before you score.

Dimension scores

One bar per rubric dimension, in the order the rubric lists them, with the weight beside it and the hub's one-sentence reason below when it gave one. An unscored dimension says "not scored" and counts as zero in the weighted score.

Human review

Where feedback is recorded. The step on recording your review covers it.

Interview timeline

Closed at first. The Slack thread the verdict came from, with the specialist panel's consults placed between the messages. The message that carried this verdict is marked. Long messages are cut short; Show full message shows the rest. Each consult card shows the domain, its signal, the number of concerns, the question asked, the concerns, and the questions the specialist suggests asking the PI. A reply cut off chip means the specialist's reply stopped before it finished. If the thread is not stored, the section says the messages are unavailable; everything above it is unaffected.

Printing

When you print the page from your browser, every collapsed section opens first, so the printout is complete.
