What it means

Your scores rate the proposal's own scientific and strategic quality, not how well the bot performed. The section asks you to read the full rationale before scoring: the brief at the top of the page is BlackbirdBot's own summary, not an independent account.

Approval status

Approval status shows No approval decision, or an earlier Approved or Disapproved with the name of the person who set it. Status history, when there is any, lists every change. No page has a control to change it. It is separate from feedback: an assessment can have feedback and no approval decision.

Feedback

Every piece of feedback recorded on the assessment is listed, oldest first, with the reviewer's name, the overall rating out of 5, the feedback mode, any dimension scores and the comment. "(edited)" marks feedback changed after it was first saved.

Add feedback

  • Overall rating (1 = weak … 5 = strong): required.
  • Rubric dimensions (optional): one score per rubric dimension. Each shows the dimension's weight and BlackbirdBot's own score for comparison, and What the scale means here explains the scale. Leave a dimension blank if you cannot judge it.
  • Comment (optional): up to 10,000 characters. Use it to explain your score and to flag anything the assessment got wrong.
  • Feedback mode: Learn unless you choose otherwise.
    • Learn marks this feedback as eligible for the prompt-suggestion bot and nothing more. {% if permissions.staff %}Suggestions are generated only when a human presses the button on the prompt-suggestions page.{% else %}A manager decides when suggestions are generated.{% endif %}
    • Don't learn — log only records the feedback and nothing else.
  • Submit feedback saves it.

Each submission adds a new piece of feedback; it does not replace your earlier one. An identical review submitted again straight away is not saved twice, and the page says so.

Quick scoring on the list

The card's Quick scoring form has the overall rating, the comment and the feedback mode, and no dimension scores. Feedback submitted from the Unreviewed tab moves that card to Reviewed: the page says "Moved to Reviewed." and moves on to the next card.

Editing and deleting

Your own feedback has an Edit control on the detail page. Saving an edit replaces that feedback's rating, dimension scores, comment and mode. You cannot edit anyone else's feedback. You cannot delete feedback: only an admin can.

Assigned

The end of the section lists who the assessment is assigned to, or "No one assigned." A manager can assign it to people, and Assigned to me on the list then finds it. An assignment is a note of who should review it; it never grants access to anything.
