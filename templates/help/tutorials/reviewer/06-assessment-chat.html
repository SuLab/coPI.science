Where

On an assessment's detail page (Workspace → Assessments, then detail → or chat on a card).

What you'll see

{{ figure(request, "chat-drawer", "The assessment chat open beside the detail page: a question, an answer with its Sources list, and the question box with Ask and Clear conversation.") }}

What it means

The chat answers questions about one assessment from that assessment's record: the verdict, the interview transcript, the specialist panel's findings, the human reviews and the scoring rubric. Fields that are not shown to your role are left out of what it reads, as they are left off the page.

Opening it

  • The round speech-bubble button at the bottom right of the detail page (its tooltip reads "Ask about this assessment").
  • The chat button on a card in the list, which opens the detail page with the chat already open.
  • An Ask: button at the end of a page section. It opens the chat and sends that question straight away.

Close or the Escape key closes it. The panel shows who you are signed in as. On a wide screen you can drag its left edge to resize it; on a narrow screen it fills the screen.

Asking

Until you have asked anything, the panel lists questions about this assessment under "Questions about this assessment. A click sends one:". A click sends that question at once. Otherwise type in the box: Enter sends, and Shift+Enter starts a new line. Under the box, a counter shows the length of your question against its limit, and a line shows how many questions you have used today against your allowance. Those limits can change, so read them in the panel rather than here.

Reading an answer

  • An answer that cites the record has a Sources list under it. Show in page next to a source scrolls to that part of the page, opens it if it was collapsed, and highlights it. On a narrow screen the chat closes so you can see it.
  • Verdict updated after this point marks where the hub updated the verdict after your earlier questions. While an interview may still be running, a note at the top of the panel warns that this can happen. Earlier questions stay visible but are no longer used as context.
  • In a long conversation, a line says when earlier turns are no longer part of what the model sees.

Clear conversation

Clear conversation deletes your whole conversation on this assessment, after asking you to confirm. It cannot be undone. It deletes only your own conversation, and it does not give back the questions you have used today.

Who can read it

Your conversation is saved for you, and the panel says so: "Saved for you; administrators with database access can read it."

When the chat is unavailable

The chat can be switched off, and then no chat controls appear.{% if permissions.admin %} It is also unavailable while an admin is viewing the app as another user; the detail page then says "Chat unavailable while impersonating".{% endif %}

More detail: The assessment chat and the chat's messages in Troubleshooting.
