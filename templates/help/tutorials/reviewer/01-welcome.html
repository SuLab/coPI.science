What it means

Blackbird screens research ideas from PIs' labs. Each PI has a lab agent, a bot that speaks for the lab in Slack. During a simulation run the lab agents pitch ideas to BlackbirdBot, the hub, which interviews each lab about its pitch. Along the way the hub can consult a panel of specialists. When an interview ends, the hub writes a verdict: a recommendation, scores against Blackbird's investment rubric, gating answers and red flags. The app stores each verdict as an assessment.

As a reviewer, you read those assessments and record your own judgement of each proposal. Your feedback rates the proposal's own merit, not how well the bot performed.

What you can reach

  • Workspace: the PIs directory and each PI's page, and the Assessments list and each assessment's detail page.
  • The assessment chat, where you can ask questions about one assessment.
  • Settings, for your own account.
  • Help, where this tutorial and the reference pages live.

What you cannot reach

The other Workspace pages are for managers: Slack Bots, Staff, Discussions, Activity and Prompt Suggestions. Opening one of them shows "Manager access required". On a PI's page you see what the PI's profile says, but not the controls a manager uses to manage the PI or the lab bot. A few fields on an assessment are shown to managers only.
