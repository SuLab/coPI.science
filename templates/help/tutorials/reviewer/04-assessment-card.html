What it means

The top of the card

  • The headline is the hub's one-line story of the proposal. Older assessments have none and show the short project label instead. The hub's confidence follows in square brackets.
  • The line below gives the Lab, which links to the PI's page, the date, and the rubric version that scored the assessment.
  • The chip on the right is the hub's recommendation: advance, conditional, route-to-incubation, or the rubric's label for a pass, the same label the band line under the page heading uses. It is the hub's own call, not the computed band.

The body of the card

  • In one minute is the hub's short pitch of the idea, shown in full.
  • Key points is a short summary in groups. Some points carry a labelled extra line, such as a risk.
  • detail → opens the assessment's detail page. chat opens the same page with the assessment chat already open. The chat button is missing when the chat is switched off.
  • Why this score, closed at first, shows the weighted score, its band and the hub's own account of why it scored that way. Older assessments may have no such account.

The footer

  • The number of red flags, or "no flags".
  • A panel badge when the specialist panel needs your attention: panel incomplete (a domain the verdict needed was never consulted), panel unverified (the check could not be made), panel not recorded (the assessment does not say whether a panel was needed), or panel state unknown. No badge means no gap was recorded or no panel was required.
  • An Approved or Disapproved chip, when an earlier approval decision exists. No page has a control that sets one.
  • Assigned: who the assessment is assigned to, and Reviewed by: who has reviewed it.

Rubric scores & gating

Closed at first. Inside, each rubric dimension has its score, a bar and its weight, or "not scored". The bars use the scale of the rubric revision that scored this assessment. Below them, each gate has a symbol, its title, and either the hub's reason or the rubric's definition of the gate:

  • met;
  • not met: asked, and failed;
  • unconfirmed: never asked. This is not a failed gate.

Quick scoring

Each card ends with a Quick scoring form: an overall rating, a comment and a feedback mode, with Submit feedback. It rates the proposal's own merit, not the bot's performance. It has no per-dimension scores; those are on the detail page. The step on recording your review explains each field.
