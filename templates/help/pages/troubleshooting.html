Troubleshooting

Messages you may see, grouped by where they appear, with what each one means and what to do. Each heading is the message as Blackbird shows it. Where a message states a limit, the heading leaves the number out (…), because the message on screen gives it.

{% call note("Error pages") %}

Some messages appear on a page of their own, under a heading such as "Access denied" or "Bad request", with Back and Home links. Others appear in a coloured box at the top of the page you were on. A few, such as "Cross-site request refused.", are plain text on an otherwise empty page.

{% endcall %}

Signing in

These appear in a red box on the sign-in page{% if microsoft_sign_in %}, or on Settings while you link a Microsoft account{% endif %}.

"Authentication was cancelled or failed. Please try again."

What it means: ORCID did not complete the sign-in, for example because you cancelled it on ORCID's page.

What to do: press Sign in with ORCID again.

"No authorization code received. Please try again."

What it means: ORCID sent you back without confirming who you are.

What to do: sign in again from the sign-in page.

"Security check failed. Please try again."

What it means: the sign-in that came back did not match the one this browser started. This happens when a sign-in page was left open too long, or a sign-in was started in another tab.

What to do: close other sign-in tabs and sign in again from the sign-in page.

"Could not complete authentication. Please try again."

What it means: Blackbird could not finish the sign-in with ORCID.

What to do: try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.

"Sign in failed. Please try again."

What it means: the sign-in failed for another reason, for example ORCID did not return an ORCID iD.

What to do: try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.

{% if microsoft_sign_in %}

"Microsoft sign-in was cancelled or not approved."

What it means: you cancelled on Microsoft's page, or did not approve the request.

What to do: press Sign in with Microsoft (staff) again, or sign in with ORCID.

"Microsoft sign-in failed. If your organisation must approve Blackbird first, ask its IT team."

What it means: Microsoft refused the sign-in. Some organisations must approve an app before their staff can use it.

What to do: sign in with ORCID meanwhile, and ask your organisation's IT team whether Blackbird needs their approval.

"Microsoft sign-in failed. Please try again or use ORCID."

What it means: Blackbird could not confirm the Microsoft sign-in, or the account is not a member account of an organisation Blackbird accepts.

What to do: try once more. If it fails again, sign in with ORCID and ask an admin whether your organisation's accounts can be used.

"Microsoft needs you to sign in again. Please try again."

What it means: Microsoft wants you to sign in to it again before it confirms who you are.

What to do: press Sign in with Microsoft (staff) again.

"No Blackbird account is linked to this Microsoft account. Sign in with ORCID, then link it in Settings."

What it means: this Microsoft account has not been linked to a Blackbird account. Your first sign-in is always with ORCID.

What to do: sign in with ORCID and link the Microsoft account in Settings (see Signing in with Microsoft).

"Microsoft sign-in is only for staff accounts. Please use ORCID."

What it means: the Microsoft account is linked to an account that may not use Microsoft sign-in.

What to do: sign in with ORCID.

The next messages appear on the Settings page while you link a Microsoft account.

"Could not confirm your ORCID sign-in. Please try again."

What it means: the ORCID sign-in you made to confirm it is you did not pass: it was for a different ORCID account, it was cancelled, or it took too long.

What to do: press Sign in with ORCID again to link and sign in with your own ORCID iD.

"Sign in with ORCID again, then link your Microsoft account."

What it means: linking needs a recent ORCID sign-in made for the purpose, and yours is missing or too old.

What to do: press Sign in with ORCID again to link, then Link Microsoft account straight away.

"That Microsoft account is already linked to a Blackbird account."

What it means: another Blackbird account uses that Microsoft account.

What to do: pick a different Microsoft account, or ask an admin.

"Unlink your current Microsoft account first."

What it means: your account already has a Microsoft account linked, and it can have only one.

What to do: press Unlink Microsoft account, sign in again, then link the other one.

Access

"Thanks for signing in" … "you're in the queue for access."

What it means: the ORCID iD you signed in with has no approved account. Usually the account was created with a different ORCID iD, or it has not been created yet.

What to do: check that you signed in with the ORCID iD your account was created with. If you did, ask the person who set up your account: {% if permissions.staff %}an admin{% else %}a manager or an admin{% endif %}.

"Access turned off"

What it means: your account's access has been turned off, and you have been signed out.

What to do: the page says who can restore it. A reviewer asks a manager or an admin; a manager asks an admin.

"Manager access required"

What it means: that page is for managers and admins only.{% if not permissions.staff %} Reviewers can open the PIs and Assessments pages, and the Help pages listed in Help.{% endif %}

What to do: {% if permissions.staff %}if you are a manager and see this, your account's role may have changed; ask an admin.{% else %}nothing: you have not done anything wrong. Go back and carry on from the PIs or Assessments page.{% endif %}

"Review access required"

What it means: the Workspace and Help are for admins, managers and reviewers, and the account you are signed in with is none of these.

What to do: check that you signed in with the ORCID iD your account was created with. If you did, ask the person who set up your account.

"Cross-site request refused."

What it means: Blackbird refused a form because your browser did not show that it came from a Blackbird page at Blackbird's usual address: for example the page was opened at a different address for Blackbird or from a saved copy, or a browser extension or privacy setting removed that information. The message is plain text on an otherwise empty page.

What to do: use your browser's Back button, open Blackbird at its usual address, and try again. If it keeps happening, try another browser and tell an admin.

"You cannot delete this account yourself."

What it means: your account holds research data (a researcher profile, publications or a lab agent), and deleting the account would delete that data too.

What to do: ask an admin to delete your account.

{% if permissions.admin %}

"Help changes are disabled while impersonating."

What it means: while viewing the site as someone else, you tried to answer their first-sign-in prompt or finish a tutorial for them. Only they can do that.

What to do: nothing; it changed nothing on their account.

Assessments

"That feedback was already recorded a moment ago; it was not saved twice."

What it means: you submitted a review identical to one you had just submitted on the same assessment, usually by pressing the button twice. The first one was saved.

What to do: nothing. To change the review, use Edit on it in the assessment's Human review section.

"Moved to Reviewed."

What it means: your review was saved from the Unreviewed tab, so the assessment now has written feedback and has moved to the Reviewed tab. The list shows the next card.

What to do: nothing; open the Reviewed tab to see it.

"score must be between 1 and 5"

What it means: the overall rating sent was outside 1 to 5.

What to do: go back, choose an overall rating from 1 to 5, and submit again.

"comment is longer than 10000 characters"

What it means: the comment is over the limit of 10,000 characters.

What to do: go back, shorten the comment, and submit again.

"comment contains a NUL character"

What it means: the comment contains an invisible control character, usually from text pasted from another program.

What to do: go back, retype the comment or paste it as plain text, and submit again.

"unknown rubric dimension: …" or "dimension score for … must be between …"

What it means: the dimension scores did not match the current rubric, usually because the rubric changed after you opened the page.

What to do: reload the assessment's page and enter the review again.

"Only the author may edit this feedback"

What it means: the review you tried to edit was written by someone else. You can edit only your own.

What to do: add a review of your own instead.

Assessment chat

These appear in red under the question box in the chat.

"The assessment chat is switched off."

What it means: the chat has been turned off for everyone.

What to do: read the assessment's page instead, and try the chat later.

{% if permissions.admin %}

"The chat is unavailable while impersonating."

What it means: while viewing the site as someone else, you cannot use the chat.

What to do: stop viewing as them to use the chat as yourself.

{% endif %}

"This assessment no longer exists."

What it means: the assessment was removed after you opened its page.

What to do: go back to the Assessments page.

"Your account can no longer use the chat. Reload the page."

What it means: your account changed while the page was open.

What to do: reload the page.

"The request was refused. Reload the page and try again."

What it means: the question was sent in a form Blackbird did not accept, usually from a page that was open for a long time.

What to do: reload the page and ask again.

"A question must not be empty and must fit the character limit shown below the box."

What it means: the question was empty or too long.

What to do: type a question, or shorten it until the count below the box is within the limit.

"This conversation is full. Clear it to start a new one."

What it means: the conversation on this assessment has reached its length limit.

What to do: press Clear conversation, which deletes your conversation on this assessment, and ask again.

"You have used today's questions." …

What it means: you have asked as many questions as one person may in a day. The rest of the message says when the limit resets; the chat also shows how many of today's questions you have used.

What to do: wait until then.

"You have reached today's spending limit for the chat."

What it means: your questions today have used up the chat's daily budget for one person.

What to do: try again tomorrow.

"The chat has reached today's spending limit for everyone."

What it means: the chat's daily budget for all users together is used up.

What to do: try again tomorrow.

"An answer is still being written. Wait for it to finish."

What it means: you asked while the previous answer was still arriving.

What to do: wait for it, then ask again.

"The model is busy. Try again in a minute."

What it means: the AI model is handling too many requests.

What to do: wait a minute and ask again.

"The model is overloaded. Try again in a minute."

What it means: the AI model is overloaded.

What to do: wait a minute and ask again.

"The model could not be reached. Try again."

What it means: Blackbird could not reach the AI model.

What to do: ask again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.

"The model stopped before finishing an answer. Try again."

What it means: the answer was cut off.

What to do: ask again.

"The answer took too long and was stopped."

What it means: the answer ran past the time allowed.

What to do: ask again, perhaps a narrower question.

"The model returned no answer. Try rephrasing."

What it means: the AI model gave an empty answer.

What to do: ask the question in other words.

"The answer could not be saved. Try again."

What it means: the answer was written but could not be stored.

What to do: ask again.

"The chat is busy. Try again in a moment."

What it means: the chat could not take your question just then.

What to do: wait a moment and ask again.

"Your session has ended — reload the page."

What it means: you were signed out, for example by signing out on another device.

What to do: reload the page and sign in again.

"The connection was lost. Reopen the chat to see whether the answer was saved."

What it means: the connection dropped while the answer was arriving.

What to do: close the chat and open it again; the saved conversation shows whether the answer arrived.

"The model rejected the request. Tell an administrator."

What it means: the AI model refused the request, which points to a problem in how the chat is set up.

What to do: tell an admin which assessment you were on.

"The chat is misconfigured (its model has no price). Tell an administrator." or "The chat is misconfigured (its instructions are missing). Tell an administrator."

What it means: the chat is not set up correctly.

What to do: tell an admin.

"Something went wrong. Try again."

What it means: an unexpected error.

What to do: ask again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.

PI pages

These appear in a red box near the top of a PI's page, after an action on that page, unless noted.

"Activation refused — this PI's profile is not ready:"

What it means: Activate agent found problems, listed under the heading. A message starting "Activation refused:" also names them.

What to do: fix what the list names (if it offers it, use Retry profile generation, after correcting the PI's name if it is wrong). Managers cannot activate past these checks. Only an admin can override the profile checks; nobody can override the persona file, account or hub checks. If a reason names the persona file, press Re-export on the PI's page.

"Activation refused: this lab could not be connected to the hub: …"

What it means: the agent passed its checks, but its hub spoke could not be set up, so it was not activated. This message appears on its own, without the list of reasons.

What to do: ask an admin to check it.

"Slack provisioning failed: …"

What it means: creating or installing the Slack bot failed; the rest of the message says why. "Install the Slack bot first." means Activate agent was pressed before the bot was installed; "the installation was cancelled in Slack" means the install was not approved on Slack's screen. When the install fails or is cancelled on Slack's screen, the message appears at the top of the PIs list instead of the PI's page. "This install was started by a different account." means the install must be finished from the account that pressed Install Slack bot.

What to do: press Install Slack bot and finish installing it in Slack, then activate.

"This agent changed while you were activating it (someone else acted first). Reload the page and check its status."

What it means: someone changed the agent at the same moment.

What to do: reload the page and check the agent's status before acting again.

"This agent can't be muted or unmuted in its current state."

What it means: only an active agent can be muted, and only a muted one unmuted.

What to do: reload the page to see its current status.

"This agent can't be unmuted until its Slack bot is installed."

What it means: the agent has no installed Slack bot.

What to do: ask an admin to check its Slack bot.

"This agent can't be unmuted yet: its profile doesn't pass the activation checks."

What it means: unmuting runs the same checks as activating, and the profile fails them.

What to do: fix the profile, then unmute again.

"This agent can't be unmuted: it could not be connected to the hub." …

What it means: its hub spoke could not be set up.

What to do: ask an admin to check it, as the message says.

"This PI has no lab agent yet, so there is nothing to mute or unmute."

What it means: the PI has no lab agent.

What to do: nothing to mute; see Adding a PI.

"Please enter a valid email address."

What it means: the email in Edit Profile is not a valid address.

What to do: correct it and save again.

"That email address is already in use by another account."

What it means: another account has that email.

What to do: check the address; each account needs its own.

"This account has no email address to verify."

What it means: Mark verified was pressed for an account with no email.

What to do: add the email in Edit Profile first.

"The address changed after this page loaded. Check it, then verify again."

What it means: the email changed between loading the page and pressing Mark verified.

What to do: check the address shown, then press Mark verified again.

"JHU tenure start must be a …-digit year (or blank to leave unchanged)."

What it means: the tenure year is not a year.

What to do: enter a year, or leave the field blank to keep the current one.

"This profile changed while you were editing it (a regeneration or another edit saved first). Reload the page to see the current version, then re-apply your changes."

What it means: the profile was saved by something else while your form was open, so your changes were not saved.

What to do: as the message says.

"This form was opened before the profile existed (or is out of date). Reload the page and make your changes again."

What it means: the form no longer matches the stored profile.

What to do: as the message says.

"Name, institution and department are limited to … characters each.", "Names may contain letters, spaces, hyphens, apostrophes, periods and commas, at most … characters.", "The research summary is limited to … words." and the list limits

What it means: a field in Edit Profile is too long, or holds characters it may not; the message gives the limit. The list limits are "Each list is limited to … items.", "Each list item is limited to … characters." and "A list item may not contain a line break or start with #."

What to do: shorten or correct the field and save again.

"There is nothing to save yet — fill in at least one field."

What it means: the form was saved empty.

What to do: fill in at least one field.

"Profile is being generated — try again shortly."

What it means: a profile generation is running, so the profile cannot be changed now.

What to do: wait for it to finish, reload, and try again.

"This PI's profile is being regenerated or updated — try again in a moment."

What it means: another change to this PI's profile is in progress.

What to do: wait a moment and try again.

"ORCID fundings are being refreshed — try the veto again in a moment."

What it means: the ORCID fundings are being refreshed, so a veto cannot be recorded now.

What to do: wait a moment, reload, and veto again.

"This PI's industry evidence is being refreshed — try the veto again in a moment."

What it means: the industry evidence is being refreshed, so a veto cannot be recorded now.

What to do: wait a moment, reload, and veto again.

"No RePORTER project lists that profile id."

What it means: the RePORTER profile id you entered matched no project.

What to do: check the id and try again.

"NIH RePORTER could not be reached — try again."

What it means: RePORTER did not answer.

What to do: try again later.

"Select a candidate or enter a RePORTER profile id."

What it means: This is the PI was pressed with nothing chosen.

What to do: tick a candidate or type a profile id, then press it again.

"PubMed could not be reached — try again."

What it means: PubMed did not answer.

What to do: try again later.

"PubMed returned no record for that PMID."

What it means: PubMed has no paper with that PMID.

What to do: check the PMID.

"There is no draft to accept."

What it means: the draft was already accepted or discarded.

What to do: reload the page.

"The profile changed since this draft was made; discard it or regenerate."

What it means: the stored profile changed after the draft was made, so accepting it would overwrite that change.

What to do: press Discard draft, or regenerate the profile.

"Regeneration refused (see the message above)."

What it means: Regenerate profile cannot be used yet. The reason is shown with the message and under the button.

What to do: wait until the reason no longer applies.

"Nothing to retry: profile generation is running, or the profile is already grounded and has a summary."

What it means: Retry profile generation has nothing to do.

What to do: reload the page to see the current state.

"This PI has no agent or profile to export."

What it means: Re-export was pressed for a PI with no lab agent or no profile, so there is no persona file to write.

What to do: nothing to export yet; the persona file is written once the PI has both.

"This account's access is denied; no profile is generated."

What it means: Regenerate profile or Retry profile generation was pressed for an account whose access is denied, so no generation was queued.

What to do: ask an admin about the account's access.

"Company discovery is already queued or running for this PI."

What it means: Find companies was pressed while a search for this PI was already waiting or running. This one appears in a blue box, not a red one.

What to do: nothing; its results appear under Suggested when it finishes.

"A profile generation is queueing discovery for this PI right now — try again in a moment."

What it means: a profile generation was starting a company search for this PI at the same moment.

What to do: wait a moment, reload, and check whether a search is already waiting before pressing Find companies again.

"Not confirmed: …"

What it means: a company was not confirmed because a field was not accepted; the rest of the message says which, for example the funding figure or its date.

What to do: correct that field and press Confirm again.

"Something went wrong saving changes."

What it means: the action failed for a reason the page has no sentence for.

What to do: reload the page and check whether the change was made. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.

Adding a PI on the PIs page can show these next to the Add PI button:

"That doesn't look like an ORCID iD (expected …)."

What it means: the ORCID iD is not in ORCID's format, or its last character (the check digit) does not match the rest of the iD, which usually means a typing error.

What to do: copy the iD from the person's ORCID record and try again.

"A user with that ORCID already exists."

What it means: the PI, or another account, already uses that ORCID iD.

What to do: look the PI up in the directory.

"Could not fetch that ORCID record — check the iD and try again."

What it means: ORCID did not return a record for that iD.

What to do: check the iD and try again.

"This ORCID record shows no public name — enter the PI's name and add again."

What it means: the ORCID record has no public name to use.

What to do: type the name in Name (only if ORCID shows none) and press Add PI again.

"Another PI was added at the same moment — please try again."

What it means: two PIs were added at once.

What to do: press Add PI again.

"Could not create the PI — see the server log."

What it means: adding the PI failed.

What to do: tell an admin.

Staff pages

These appear at the top of the Staff page after an action there.

"Choose a role you may create."

What it means: the role chosen is not one you may create. Managers create reviewer accounts only.

What to do: choose Reviewer, or ask an admin to create the account.

"That doesn't look like an ORCID iD (expected …)."

What it means: the ORCID iD is not in ORCID's format, or its last character (the check digit) does not match the rest of the iD, which usually means a typing error.

What to do: copy the iD from the person's ORCID record and try again.

"Could not fetch that ORCID record. Check the iD and try again."

What it means: ORCID did not return a record for that iD.

What to do: check the iD and try again.

"This ORCID record shows no public name. Enter the person's name and try again."

What it means: the ORCID record has no public name to use.

What to do: type the person's name and preview again.

"Names may contain letters, spaces, hyphens, apostrophes, periods and commas (at most … characters)."

What it means: the name has a character it may not, or is too long.

What to do: correct the name and try again.

"That ORCID iD already belongs to an account that cannot be converted here."

What it means: the ORCID iD belongs to an account the Staff page cannot turn into a staff account.

What to do: ask an admin.

"That ORCID iD already belongs to an account. Ask an admin to change it."

What it means: the ORCID iD belongs to an account only an admin can change, for example a PI with a lab agent or a profile. A manager also sees it when an admin has already approved that ORCID iD for access, even if no account exists yet.

What to do: ask an admin to change the account, or to create it.

"That account has profile or enrichment work running. Try again when it finishes."

What it means: jobs are running for that account.

What to do: try again later.

"Something changed since the preview. Review it again before confirming."

What it means: the account or the ORCID record changed between Preview and Confirm.

What to do: read the new preview, then confirm.

"That account no longer exists."

What it means: the account was deleted.

What to do: reload the page.

"Only reviewer accounts can be changed here."

What it means: Revoke, Restore and Delete on the Staff page work on reviewer accounts only.

What to do: ask an admin to change a manager or admin account.

"You cannot change your own account here."

What it means: the Staff page does not act on your own account.

What to do: ask an admin.

"This reviewer has never signed in. Delete the unused account instead, or ask an admin."

What it means: a manager cannot revoke a reviewer who has never signed in.

What to do: delete the unused account, or ask an admin to revoke it.

"This account holds research data (a researcher profile, publications or a lab agent). Ask an admin to delete it."

What it means: the account holds a researcher profile, publications or a lab agent, which a delete would destroy.

What to do: ask an admin.

"That reviewer's access is not active."

What it means: the reviewer's access is not currently approved, so there is nothing to revoke.

What to do: reload the page.

"That reviewer's access is not revoked."

What it means: Restore was pressed for a reviewer whose access is not turned off.

What to do: reload the page.

"Could not complete that. See the server log."

What it means: the action failed.

What to do: tell an admin.

"Staff account changes are disabled while impersonating."

"Review actions are disabled while impersonating."

What to do: nothing; no change was made.
