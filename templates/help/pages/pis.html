PIs

A PI is a principal investigator whose lab takes part in Blackbird. The PIs page lists them, and each PI has a page with their research profile, grants, industry interest, companies and publications. These pages give you the background to the labs behind the assessments you review. See How Blackbird works for how PIs and their lab agents fit together.

The PI directory

The directory lists PIs by name, with the total number of PIs at the top right. When the list runs to more than one page, ← Prev and Next → at the bottom move between pages. Click a PI's name, or anywhere on their row, to open their page.

{{ figure(request, "pis-list-reviewer", "The PI directory as a reviewer sees it.") }}

The columns everyone sees:

Name
The PI's name, with their department under it when it is recorded.
Institution
The PI's institution, or a dash.
ORCID
The PI's ORCID iD, which opens their ORCID record in a new tab.
Pubs (tenure)
How many of the PI's publications fall inside their JHU tenure window. When no JHU tenure start is recorded, the column counts the whole career and says full career. Publications with no recorded year cannot be placed in the window, so they are left out of the count, and the column notes how many are undated.
Industry interest
The PI's industry-interest cohort percentile: how their industry evidence ranks against the other scored PIs. A dash means there is no score to show.

On a narrow screen some of these columns are hidden; open a PI's page to see everything.

What managers and admins also see

Staff get more columns:

  • Status: the profile status, No Profile, Generating, Complete or Pending draft. See Profile status.
  • Agent: the lab agent's status, Not requested, Pending, Active, Muted or Suspended.
  • Version: the profile version.
  • Claimed: the date a sign-in claimed the account, or "No". A PI added by a manager claims it at their first sign-in; a PI who created their own account by signing in, at their second.
  • Joined and Last Login.

In the Industry interest column, staff also see why a PI has no percentile ("rescoring" or "unscored"), and "partial" when some evidence sources were unavailable.

The filter bar above the table has Profile Status and Claimed (Claimed or Unclaimed) menus; press Apply.

A PI's page

← All PIs at the top returns to the directory. The page shows these sections.

{{ figure(request, "pi-detail-reviewer", "The top of a PI's page as a reviewer sees it: Details, Research Profile and the start of Grants.") }}
Details
The PI's Keywords and JHU tenure start. A year marked "provisional" was inferred from an incomplete record and has not been confirmed.
Research Profile
Shown once the PI's profile has been generated. It has the profile's version, the research summary, and the Techniques, Disease Areas, Model Systems and Key Targets it lists.
Grants

The RePORTER identity line says whether the PI's NIH RePORTER profile has been identified: "resolved", "pinned by staff", "staff confirmed: no RePORTER profile", "not evaluated yet", or another state with "no RePORTER grants are shown". The line under it says how many active and past grants the lab agent's description of the PI includes. Past grants are not shown when no JHU tenure start is recorded.

NIH RePORTER awards lists each award's title, project number, activity code and fiscal years, and how it was linked to this PI. ORCID fundings lists the funding the PI's ORCID record shows. A greyed entry marked "vetoed" was ruled out by staff and is kept out of the lab agent's description.

Industry interest
The PI's cohort percentile, the raw score behind it and how many evidence rows it counts, or "No score available." Evidence opens the list of industry evidence: the company, the kind of tie, the year, the PI's role and the source, with the quoted text where there is one. Greyed entries marked "vetoed" were ruled out by staff.
Companies
Companies the PI founded or co-founded, advises, or serves on the board of, as confirmed by staff. Each entry shows the PI's role, whether it was added by hand or discovered, when it was confirmed, any funding figure, and links to its Source. Confirmed companies reach BlackbirdBot's profile lookup and also appear on the assessment pages.
Publications
The PI's publications inside their JHU tenure window. The heading says the year the window starts, or "full career — no JHU tenure start recorded". Each publication shows its journal, year, whether its methods text was found ("Methods ✓" or "No methods"), and a PubMed link where there is one. Undated publications are excluded and counted under the heading. Publications from before the tenure window are not listed anywhere.

The manager-only parts of a PI's page

Managers and admins see the same sections with controls added, and these extra cards:

  • the lab agent's status and its button in the page header (Install Slack bot, Activate agent, Mute or Unmute): see Installing the Slack bot, Activation and Muting;
  • the Account card: see The Account card;
  • Edit Profile, which replaces the Details card once a profile exists: see Editing a profile;
  • Corpus and profile review, with the pending draft and the candidate, unanchored and excluded papers: see Reviewing papers and Drafts;
  • Retry profile generation, when generation needs another try: see Regenerating a profile;
  • Persona revisions: see The persona;
  • the RePORTER identity controls and the Not this PI vetoes under Grants: see RePORTER identity, Grants and ORCID fundings;
  • the industry score's details and the evidence veto: see Industry interest;
  • suggested companies, Add a company, Delete and Find companies: see Companies;
  • the Jobs table: see Jobs.
