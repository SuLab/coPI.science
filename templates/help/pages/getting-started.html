Getting started

How to sign in, where you land, how to find your way around the Workspace and Help, what Settings holds, and what to do when Blackbird will not let you in. Words you have not met before are in the Glossary.

Signing in

Blackbird identifies you by your ORCID iD. On the sign-in page, press Sign in with ORCID, sign in on ORCID's own page, and ORCID sends you back to Blackbird. Use the ORCID iD your account was created with: a different iD is a different account.

If you followed a link to a Blackbird page before you were signed in, that page opens as soon as you have signed in. Otherwise you land on your usual first page (see Your first sign-in).

If the sign-in page shows a red message instead, look it up under Troubleshooting: signing in.

Signing in with Microsoft

Managers and reviewers can also sign in with their organisation's Microsoft account, as a second way in. ORCID keeps working. Microsoft works only once you have linked it to your account, so your first sign-in is always with ORCID. Only a member account of an organisation Blackbird accepts can be used, not a guest account.

Linking your Microsoft account

  1. Sign in with ORCID and open Settings.
  2. In the Microsoft sign-in section, press Sign in with ORCID again to link. ORCID asks you to sign in again, to confirm it is you, and sends you back to Settings.
  3. Press Link Microsoft account straight away and pick your Microsoft account. The link must follow soon after that ORCID sign-in; if you wait too long, Settings asks you to sign in with ORCID again first.
  4. Settings confirms: "Microsoft sign-in linked. You can now use it on the sign-in page." It then shows the linked account, when you linked it, and when you last used it.

Signing in with Microsoft

On the sign-in page, press Sign in with Microsoft (staff) and pick the Microsoft account you linked. You land where an ORCID sign-in would take you.

Unlinking

In Settings, Unlink Microsoft account removes the link. It asks you to confirm first, and then signs you out on every device, this one included. Sign in again with ORCID afterwards; you can link a Microsoft account again at any time.

Your first sign-in

Managers and reviewers have no lab and no research profile of their own, so there is no profile set-up to go through: you go straight into the Workspace. A manager lands on the PIs page and a reviewer on the Assessments page, unless sign-in is taking you back to a page you were trying to open.

Until you answer it, each Workspace page shows a box at the top: New to Blackbird? It offers the tutorial for your role, a read-only tour of the pages you will use.

{{ figure(request, "intro-prompt", "The box on a Workspace page that offers your role's tutorial.") }}
  • Start the tutorial opens its first step. The box stays on Workspace pages until you press Finish on the tutorial's last step.
  • Don't show again hides the box for good. You stay on the page you were on, with the note "The tutorials are always under Help."

Don't show again, or Finish on the tutorial's last step, hides the box for good; Start the tutorial records nothing. You can take any tutorial for your role from Help at any time.

Finding your way around

The bar along the top of every page holds:

  • Settings: your account (see Settings below);
  • Workspace: the pages you work in. It opens on {% if permissions.staff %}PIs{% else %}Assessments{% endif %};
  • Help: these pages and the tutorials;
  • your name, and Sign out.
{{ figure(request, "topbar-help", "The top bar, with Help beside Workspace.") }}

Inside the Workspace, a second row of links moves between its pages. Everyone sees:

  • PIs: the PI directory and each PI's page. See PIs.
  • Assessments: BlackbirdBot's screening verdicts, where reviews are recorded. See Assessments.

  • Slack Bots: every PI lab bot and where it is in Slack onboarding. See The Slack Bots page.
  • Staff: adding reviewers and turning their access off and on. See Staff accounts.
  • Discussions and Activity: the simulation's threads and its runs. See Discussions and Activity.
  • Interviews: each simulation interview, one line per post. See Interviews.
  • Prompt Suggestions: prompt changes proposed from review feedback. See Prompt suggestions.

Most Workspace pages have a Help with this page link in their header, which opens the matching part of Help. In Help itself, the row of links under the top bar lists the Overview, the tutorials for your role and every Help page you can open.

Settings

Settings holds your account.

  • Account has the Delete account link.

  • Microsoft sign-in links or unlinks a Microsoft account (see Signing in with Microsoft).
    • {% endif %}
{% call careful("Deleting your account is permanent") %}

Delete account opens a page that lists what deleting your account removes and what it keeps. To go ahead you type the word delete and press Delete My Account; you are then signed out. Nothing in Blackbird can undo it. Cancel takes you back to Settings. If your account holds research data (a researcher profile, publications or a lab agent), the page says "You cannot delete this account yourself." instead, and only an admin can delete it.

{% endcall %}

Signing out

Sign out, at the top right, signs you out on every device and browser where you are signed in, not only this one. You will need to sign in again everywhere.

Some changes to your account sign you out everywhere in the same way: a change to your account's role, your access being turned off{% if microsoft_sign_in %}, and unlinking your Microsoft account{% endif %}.

When you can't get in

"Thanks for signing in" and a place in the queue

The page says Blackbird is in pre-release and that you are in the queue for access. It means the ORCID iD you signed in with has no approved account. Check that you used the ORCID iD your account was created with. If you did, ask the person who set up your account: {% if permissions.staff %}an admin{% else %}a manager or an admin{% endif %}.

"Access turned off"

Your account's access has been turned off, and you are signed out. The page says who can restore it: for a reviewer, a manager or an admin; for a manager, an admin.

"Cross-site request refused."

Blackbird refused a form because your browser did not show that it came from a Blackbird page at Blackbird's usual address: for example the page was opened at a different address for Blackbird or from a saved copy, or a browser extension or privacy setting removed that information. The message is plain text on an otherwise empty page. Use your browser's Back button, open Blackbird at its usual address, and try again. If it keeps happening, try another browser and tell an admin.
